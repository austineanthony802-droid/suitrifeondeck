Booking Info

Your Account

Getting Started

  • All guests attending need their own account in order to be officially added to the reservation, make payments, and check in for the cruise.
  • The Account is your personal profile, a hub for your reservation, and a place to manage your trip.

Is there an age restriction?

  • Yes, Suite Rife on Deck is an 18+ event, and at least one person per cabin must be 21 or older. Guests under 18 are not permitted onboard.

 

How do I book a cabin?

Step 1: Select the number of adults staying in your cabin.

  • Cabins are available for occupancies of 1, 2, 3, or 4 adults. You will be able to add your cabinmate(s) during booking.

Step 2: Select your cabin by deck or by category.

  • If you know what cabin number or what deck you want, choose the deck from the list to see all available cabins on that deck.
  • If you are not sure choose the category type and then check out which decks have the category you are looking for.
  • Be sure to have a few back up choices in case your first choice is sold out.

Step 3: Add a Carbon Offset. 

  • By purchasing a Carbon Offset, you are offsetting the emissions from your personal travel to the event (your carbon footprint). These support Trees, Water & People and offer an opportunity to reduce your environmental impact.
  • These items can be added during the booking process or at a later date based on availability.

Step 4: Review your reservation. 

  • You can remove add-ons by clicking the word “Remove” below the item. Hit “Check Out” once your reservation is all set.

Step 5: Add your cabinmates(s).

  • You will see your information listed as Guest 1 (aka the “Lead Guest”). Below, add your cabinmate(s) information for Guest 2. If you do not know who your cabinmate(s) will be at the time of booking, no worries! Check the box “Will provide name later” but note you will still need to cover their deposit at the time of booking. Please see the name addition/change fee schedule if choosing not to add a specific cabinmate at the time of booking.

Step 6: Choose how to pay. 

  • You can choose to pay for the entire reservation or split payment with your cabinmate(s). Choose to pay in full or take advantage of our automatic monthly payment plan for a one-time $25 fee. A credit card is required for all payments.

Step 7: Accept Terms & Conditions.

  • Enter your card information and acknowledge automatic billing if you are joining the payment plan.

Step 8: Protect your reservation.

  • After you complete your booking, you will be directed to a page which allows you to purchase travel insurance. You are welcome to purchase insurance from anyone, but we’ve done the legwork for you. We have partnered with IMG to provide plans that cover the per person cost of your reservation. To ensure you are fully covered, you will want each guest to have their own plan purchased.

** You will be sent an email later this fall with a booking date and time to select your seat for Matt’s theater shows.

What if I have accessibility needs?

  • If you need accommodations with specific ADA cabin amenities (such as toilet height, bed height, door widths, a roll-in shower, etc.), please contact the Accessibility Coordinator as far in advance as possible.
  • If you have mobility issues but do not need a roll-in shower, please make note of your mobility needs in your medical notes of your account and also contact the Accessibility Coordinator as early as possible.
  • Wheelchairs, mobility scooters, and amphibious beach chairs (designed to travel over sand and into the water) can be rented and delivered to the port through Special Needs At Sea.
  • Contact the Accessibility Coordinator below with any questions or concerns.

Payment Plan

How much does it cost to make a reservation?

  • Packages are priced per person and in USD.
  • A $250 non-refundable deposit ($500 for a Yacht Club) per person is required to reserve a cabin. This deposit goes towards the total cost of your reservation.
  • All payments must be paid by Credit Card (Visa, MasterCard, Discover & American Express).

Can I split the cost with my roommate?

  • Yes! At the time of booking, there will be an option to have 100% financial responsibility or split the financial responsibility with your guest(s).
  • If you are splitting financial responsibility, each person must have their own account.
  • The Lead Guest will first need to pay their deposit to make the reservation.
  • Following immediately after, the additional guest(s) on the reservation will need to log into their Account(s) to supply the remaining deposit(s) to confirm the reservation.
    • All deposits must be paid within 24 hours or the reservation is subject to cancellation.
  • Financial responsibility can also be split after the reservation is booked by contacting the Travel Team via email.

What are the payment plan options?

You can either pay in full or enroll in our automatic monthly payment plan. You will have five monthly payments processed on the 19th of each month starting in August and ending in December.

  • A one-time $25 payment plan fee will be added to your initial deposit payment for joining the payment plan.
  • If your payment declines you will have 48 hours to make the payment. If we have not received the payment after the 48 hour grace period, you will be assessed a $35 declined payment fee.

What is Travel Insurance and why should I get it?

We strongly suggest guests purchase travel insurance to protect your vacation investment and get peace of mind. If we have learned anything from the past it’s that you never know what can happen, it’s important to be prepared for anything.  

There are many Travel Insurance options that can be found with a quick Google search, but we have done the leg work for you and have partnered with IMG (International Medical Group) to offer Travel Insurance plan options. Click below to compare the different policies and get quotes. You purchase your policy directly with IMG, any corrections or changes must be directed to them within 10 days of your policy purchase. Please be sure to read your policy as soon as you receive it by email.

You can add insurance to your reservation at the time of booking or anytime up to 24 hours before you depart for Suite Rife on Deck, but keep in mind you MUST purchase a plan within 20 days of your initial deposit if you want Cancel For Any Reason coverage, if you have a pre-existing condition, or if you think you may need to cancel because of work.  If you purchase a policy through IMG, you have a 10 day look period and can cancel for a full refund during those 10 days.  Be sure to read through your policy when you receive it.

Reservation Policies

The cabin type I wanted sold out…now what?

    • Log into your Account to indicate a cabin preference on the Upgrade Form by selecting the FAQ’s + Upgrade Doc button.
    • The Travel Team will contact you if anything becomes available between now and the event.
    • Should an upgrade become available, you will be required to pay the cost difference between cabin types.
  • Please note, we do not allow for any cabin downgrades; however, you can upgrade your cabin if there is availability.

What if I can no longer attend?

  • All payments for Suite Rife on Deck are non-refundable. We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance to cover your vacation investment.
  • You have the option to cancel your reservation or transfer it to another person.
  • If you choose to sell your cabin reservation, it is your responsibility to find a buyer and sort out the finances.
  • Log into your Account for details on how to transfer or cancel your reservation.

How do I add or change my guest?

  • The Lead Guest is the only person on the reservation that can initiate changes, such as adding a guest or making a name change.
  • To replace a guest or add a name on the reservation, the Lead Guest must contact the Travel Team.
  • Please have everyone in your reservation create their own Account.

What are the fees to add or change a name on my reservation?

Change the Lead Guest:

  • $150 fee through November 13
  • $225 fee from November 14 – December 21, 2026

Add or Change Guest Name(s):

  • Complimentary through October 16
  • $125 fee from October 17 – November 13
  • $225 fee from November 14 – December 21, 2026

Starting December 22, 2026, no name changes will be accepted.

What is the Cancellation Policy?

  • All deposits and payments for Suite Rife on Deck are non-refundable. All orders are subject to the Suite Rife on Deck and 100x Purchase Policy, Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy.
  • The Event HIGHLY recommends that all guests purchase Travel Insurance that includes Cancel for Any Reason coverage.
  • Cancellations are only accepted from the Lead Guest with the understanding that the entire reservation will be cancelled.
  • If one person in your reservation needs to cancel or will not be attending, please contact the Travel Team to let them know and to receive instructions if you are filing an insurance claim.
  • All cancellations must be submitted in writing by completing the Cancellation Form located in your Account.
  • You are also able to transfer your reservation to another person, per the name change guidelines above.

Travel FAQ

What travel documents do I need to board the ship?

The Western Hemisphere Travel Initiative allows U.S. citizens sailing on cruises that begin and end in the same U.S. port to travel with one of the following documents:

  • Valid U.S. Passport valid 6 months beyond Suite Rife on Deck dates
  • Passport Card
  • Original copy of a state issued Birth Certificate. If your Birth Certificate has footprints on it, it will not be accepted (these are hospital issued certificates) and government photo I.D.
  • If you are a recent U.S. citizen and have not yet received your U.S. Passport, you will need to bring your original Certificate of Naturalization.
  • Original Green Card (no copies)
  • If you are traveling on a Student Visa, you should not book Suite Rife on Deck at the end of your Visa. If you are at the end of your Visa, you must provide written proof of employment or risk being left on shore!
  • * If you are not a U.S. Citizen, call U.S. Immigrations at 1-800-375-5283 for guidelines on acquiring the proper identification for your nationality.

Please note that the name on your ticket must match the name on your identification. If you are recently married or have undergone a name change, be sure to bring along supporting documentation. Contact the Travel Team to update the name on your reservation.

Failure to possess the required travel documents may prevent you from being able to travel; in such event, no refunds shall be provided to you.

If you have questions regarding your travel documents, you should contact Customs and Border Protection.

Pro Tip: Take a photo of your state issued Birth Certificate on your phone before you leave. It must be clearly legible & you must be able to see the borders so it looks like a copy when printed. This way if anything happens to your Passport, you will have a copy of your Birth Certificate.

Is airfare included in the trip cost?

  • No. All guests are responsible for booking their own airfare.

What airport should I fly into?

  • Miami Airport (MIA) is about 9 miles away (around 15-20 mins).
  • Fort Lauderdale Airport (FLL) is about 27 miles away (around 40 mins).
  • West Palm Beach Airport (PBI) is about 71 miles away (around 80+ mins).

Pro Tip:  MSC offers transportation to Miami Airport after disembarkation. You can pre-purchase transportation back to the airport when you check in online for the cruise prior to sailing and also at the Excursion desk onboard.

When and where do we get on the ship?

  • Suite Rife on Deck departs from PortMiami located in Miami, FL.
  • More information confirming the terminal will come with the Welcome Letter closer to sailing.
  • Check-in will begin at 12:00 PM ET on February 10, 2027.
  • Check-in will close at 4:00 PM ET.
  • Please plan to be on the ship no later than 4:00 PM ET on February 10.

Members of my party are arriving separately, can we board at different times?

  • Absolutely! Everyone checks in individually, so you can check in and board at separate times.

When and where do we get off the ship?

  • Suite Rife on Deck returns to PortMiami on February 14, 2027.
  • Disembarkation is scheduled to begin at 7:00 AM on the day we arrive.
  • If you are flying out of Miami, MSC recommends choosing one that departs after 12:30 PM.
  • If flying from Fort Lauderdale, MSC recommends flights that take off after 1:30 PM.
  • Book at your own discretion – Suite Rife on Deck and MSC Cruise Line are not responsible for any missed flights.

What should I pack?

Passport • Sunglasses • Hat • Sunscreen & Aloe • Bug Spray • Reusable Bottle/Cup/Straw • Rain Gear • Warm Clothes • Swimsuite • Pool Float • Ear Plugs • Sea-Bands or Dramamine • Costumes for Theme Nights

Do Not Pack These…

  • Illegal substances, weapons, drones, and pets.
  • The use of illegal drugs is strictly prohibited and all laws pertaining to drug use must be respected.

Can I bring my camera?

  • You may bring any kind of camera onboard to shoot your vacation.
  • Filming Suite Rife on Deck sets and any kind of photography during the shows is prohibited.
  • Drones are banned from the event.

Do I need an electrical converter?

  • All cabins have a few standard 120V outlets as used the United States. For more flexibility, be sure to bring a European converter.

Do I need to bring cash?

  • Purchases aboard will be made with your cruise card. If you would like to have cash for our port stops, packing a few dollars will save you time searching for an ATM. Additionally, MSC Guest Services can disperse cash to you via charging the card on account for a fee.

Are there any additional fees?

  • There is a $295 fee applied per person which includes all taxes, fees and gratuities.
  • If you are utilizing the payment plan, there is one-time $25 fee.
  • All taxes and fees are included at the time of booking, with exception of the potential fuel surcharge.

Potential Fuel Surcharge

  • MSC reserves the right to charge a fuel supplement charge in the instance that MSC implements such a charge fleet-wide for all cruises (not just specific to Suite Rife on Deck).
  • The charge will not exceed US $50.00 per person, per day.

Ship FAQ

Are food and drinks included?

  • Yes! Your reservation package includes meals in the main buffet and select restaurants where you can enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late night bites.
  • There are also up-charge dining options available at specialty restaurants onboard.
  • Non-carbonated beverages (water, non-speciality coffee, tea, juices, etc.) are included.
  • Drink packages as advertised on MSC are not available for Suite Rife on Deck. Drink tickets will be available for purchase on board. More information will be included in the Welcome Letter you will receive via email closer to the sailing.
  • Yacht Club reservations come with complimentary alcoholic beverages too (maximum of 15 drinks/day)!

Is there a dress code on the ship?

  • There is no dress code, but shirts and shoes are required in all dining areas.
  • Suite Rife on Deck will feature different theme nights, so you’ll want to pack a special costume for those too!

Will vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free options be available?

  • Vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free options will be available at all meals.
  • You should tell the host of the restaurant, and they will let your waiter know.
  • If you haven’t already, update the Dietary Request section of “Your Profile” in your Account.

What are some of the Poesia’s amenities?

Full Service Spa & Salon • Casino Royal • Specialty Restaurants & Buffet • Multiple Bars, Lounges & Pop-Up Bars • Fully Equipped Fitness Center • Multi-purpose Sports Court • Virtual Game Room

How do I make a spa appointment?

  • You will be able to purchase spa packages as a pre-purchase when you check in online before the cruise and during the sailing.
  • We recommend swinging by the spa early on Embarkation Day to reserve your appointment time.

Is there Wi-Fi on the ship?

  • Wireless access is available throughout the ship.
  • You can purchase an internet package for 1-4 devices during online check-in with MSC prior to sailing or while onboard.

Is there a Lost and Found?

  • Lost & Found is located at the MSC Help Desk in the on Deck 5.
  • If you leave something behind on the ship, contact MSC’s Lost and Found.

What in-cabin amenities are there?

Interactive TV • Electronic Safe  •  Telephone • Hair Dryer  • Complimentary Hair & Body Wash • Vanity Area

Where can I keep my valuables?

  • Every cabin on the ship has an electronic safe.
  • We strongly suggest placing your passport, wallet, cash, and any other valuables inside as soon as you arrive onboard.
  • The event is not responsible for any lost or stolen items.

Are there any medical facilities on board?

  • In case of emergency, call the Front Desk.
  • A registered nurse with regular office hours is onboard.
  • In the event of a medical emergency, a qualified medical doctor is on call 24 hours a day.
  • Guests with any pertinent medical information or specific medication needs are asked to notify us in advance.
  • Event Safety is also available 24 hours a day with US licensed EMTs.

In case of an emergency back home, how can I be contacted onboard?

  • You will have the ability to add Wi-Fi to stay connected during your trip. iMessage and WhatsApp are both great ways for your family and friends to stay in touch while you are away.
  • The Suite Rife on Deck team can be reached at customerservice@suiterifeondeck.com and will try to help communicate.
