Cabin Pricing

  • All listed cabin rates are per person.
  • A $250 per person non-refundable deposit ($500 per person for Yacht Club) is due at the time of booking.
  • Payments can be made using Visa, MasterCard, Discover & American Express.
  • There is a $295 fee applied per person which includes all taxes, fees, and gratuities for staff around the ship. 
  • Calculate your cost: If you book a Category 1 Double Occupancy $999 + $295 port fee = $1,294 per person x 2 adults = $2,588 total for the cabin.
  • Pay in full or enroll in our automatic monthly payment plan for a one-time $25 fee. You will then have five monthly payments processed on the 19th of each month starting in August and ending in December.
  • Suite Rife on Deck does not permit downgrades (going down in occupancy or category). However, you are able to upgrade your cabin based on availability.
  • Guests with ADA needs should make sure their requirements are listed in your Account and contact the Travel Team for options. 
  • There are Single Occupancy cabins available at single rates in select categories.
  • You will be sent an email later this fall with a booking date and time to select your seat for Matt’s theater shows.
